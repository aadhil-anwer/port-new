[P1] Strongest proof (CVSS 9.1/8.8/9.6, THM Top 3%, 5x CompTIA) only in JSON-LD, invisible to human hiring manager. Fix: surface 3-4 scannable credibility facts under hero (mono tags, not metric-block). Command: craft / bolder.

[P2] columns-2 runs on one column (writeups commented out), likely dead right gutter on wide screens. Fix: collapse to single column or fill. Command: layout.
